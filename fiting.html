Premium CP Bathroom Fittings Shine • Strength • Style

Elegant • Long Lasting

CP Fittings stand for Chrome Plated Fittings, a commonly used term in the plumbing and construction industry. These fittings are widely used in modern bathrooms and kitchens.

Chrome-plated bathroom fittings are popular in India for their durability, low maintenance, and stylish appearance. They reflect fine taste and give bathrooms a modern, premium appeal.

Chrome is lightweight, affordable, corrosion-resistant, and does not oxidize easily, making it the ideal choice for faucets, taps, and other deluxe sanitary fixtures.

Why Choose CP Fittings?

Strong, corrosion-resistant, water-stain resistant and available in jaw-dropping designs with spectacular shine.
